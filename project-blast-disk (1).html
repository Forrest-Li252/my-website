Process

02 / DESIGN THROUGH TEST
STEP 01

Sizing

Hand-calculated burst pressure against membrane theory to bracket a target disk thickness before modeling.

STEP 02

FEA

Ran nonlinear FEA to refine geometry and confirm the disk fails at the intended pressure, not before or after.

STEP 03

Bench Test

Pressure-tested prototype disks to burst on a bench rig, comparing measured burst pressure to the FEA prediction.

STEP 04

Integration

Machined and installed the final disk into the propulsion stack ahead of a static fire or flight.
